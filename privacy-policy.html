Privacy Policy — Orbit Lock

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Orbit Lock ("the app"), developed by NorthDune Studios ("we", "us"), handles information. We have kept the app simple: there are no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app chat.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information on our own servers. We do not operate any servers for the app.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels unlocked, stars, coins) and settings (sound and vibration) are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset progress.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads),
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type, operating system and language,
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them.

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. Anywhere, you can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Security and retention

Because we store no personal data ourselves, we have nothing to retain. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
